Founders Celebration

Biennial Celebration of our Founders in Athol, MA, honoring the work of Sophia B. Packard & Harriet E. Giles.

Here, we, the Boston Area Chapter of the National Alumnae Association of Spelman College (NAASC), commemorate the visionary founders of Spelman College and gather in advocacy, remembrance, and sisterhood.

This three-part Founders Celebration Series honors the legacy of Sophia B. Packard and Harriet E. Giles through civic engagement, reflection, and community connection: Legacy Brunch, Worship & Reflection Gathering, and Commemorative Ceremony.

All alumnae, family, friends, and supporters are welcome to attend as we gather in community to celebrate our history and continue the work of our founders.
