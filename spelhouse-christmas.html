SpelHouse Christmas Party

Celebrate the holiday season with NAASC Boston and our Morehouse Brothers at our annual SpelHouse Christmas Party! Bringing together Spelman alumnae, friends, and supporters from across the Greater Boston area, this festive gathering is an opportunity to reconnect, celebrate sisterhood, and create lasting memories.

Enjoy an evening of holiday cheer, meaningful fellowship, and the special bond that unites the SpelHouse community. Whether reconnecting with familiar faces or meeting fellow Spelman women for the first time, join us.
