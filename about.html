About Us

The National Alumnae Association of Spelman College is the official organization that supports Spelman College, a historically Black college for women, through financial contributions, student recruitment, sisterhood, community service, and the recognition of Spelman women.

The Boston Area Chapter was revitalized in 2006 and again in 2019 to meet the needs of nearly 200 Spelman alumnae in the region. The chapter sponsors events, engages with the community, and serves as a representative of Spelman College in the Greater Boston area. We are committed to informing prospective students, families, and the broader community about the benefits of a Spelman education.

The Boston Area Chapter also places a strong emphasis on building impactful relationships with local institutions and organizations. Through these partnerships, we seek to be a resource for the Boston community while identifying ways to celebrate Spelman alumnae who are making a difference in the area. We believe in uplifting one another and recognizing the achievements of local alumnae who serve our community. Together, we aim to celebrate, support, and inspire each other while contributing to the growth and development of both our alumnae and the community at large.
