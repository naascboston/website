Bound in Blue Scholarship

Program Purpose

The Bound in Blue Scholarship is a Boston-based alumnae initiative designed to provide strategic, short-term financial support to Spelman College students with demonstrated need and strong ties to the Boston community.

This is a gap scholarship that is supplemental in nature and intended to help close temporary financial shortfalls that may otherwise disrupt a scholar’s academic continuity, campus engagement, or leadership trajectory.

Beyond financial assistance, Bound in Blue is structured as a leadership and engagement program that intentionally connects scholars to NAASC Boston, the Blueprint Conference, and the broader alumnae network.

Mission Statement

The Bound in Blue Scholarship exists to close short-term financial gaps for Boston-connected Spelman College students while cultivating sustained engagement between scholars and NAASC Boston alumnae.

Grounded in Spelman’s enduring commitment to academic excellence, service, and sisterhood, this program strengthens the relationship between student and alumnae, ensuring that financial support is paired with mentorship, visibility, and leadership opportunity.

Vision

Our vision is to build a repeatable, chapter-driven model of scholar investment that:

  • Protects students from financial interruption
  • Deepens scholar–alumnae connection
  • Strengthens Blueprint Conference participation
  • Expands leadership development opportunities
  • Creates a sustainable pipeline from student to engaged alumna

Over time, Bound in Blue aims to become NAASC Boston’s signature scholar investment initiative that reinforces both financial access and lifelong sisterhood.
