Blueprint Girls Conference

The NAASC Boston Blueprint Conference is an empowering experience designed to educate, inspire, and uplift girls ages 14–18 through leadership development, mentorship, and community connection.

The Blueprint Conference: She Owns the Algorithm is a transformative gathering dedicated to inspiring and equipping local high school girls of color to lead, create, and thrive, and to learn about Spelman College. The conference brings together students, educators, and community leaders for a day of empowerment, mentorship, and sisterhood.

Through workshops, performances, and dialogue, participants explore leadership, identity, wellness, and creative expression, along with civic engagement, public speaking, confidence-building, digital media literacy, step, and social media etiquette. They learn from experts, professionals, and influencers in Boston and beyond, building confidence and community that lasts well beyond the event.
