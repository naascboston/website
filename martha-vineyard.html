Martha’s Vineyard: Spelman Sisters’ Week

Our Primary Objectives

  • Spelman Strong Fund: Raise funds to support Spelman College and its institutional priorities.
  • Educational Opportunities: Fund the Blueprint Girls Conference and provide local scholarships to support young women pursuing higher education.
  • College Recruitment: Increase awareness of Spelman College and encourage talented young women from Martha’s Vineyard to consider Spelman.
  • Community Service: Support local organizations, initiatives, and programs that address the needs of the Martha’s Vineyard community.
  • Community Partnerships: Build relationships with Vineyard businesses, nonprofits, schools, and civic organizations.
  • Youth Empowerment: Create opportunities for mentorship, leadership development, and educational enrichment for young women across the Island.
  • Alumnae Engagement: Strengthen the presence and involvement of Spelman alumnae on Martha’s Vineyard through fellowship, service, and community leadership.
