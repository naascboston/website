2027 Membership Dues, NAASC Boston Chapter. Your dues support our scholarships, service, and sisterhood. Dues renew yearly on July 2.

C’2026

Free

The most recent graduating class receives a one-year complimentary membership.

C’2021 – 2025

$75

National dues are $25 and local dues are $50. Recent alumnae whose class has not yet celebrated its first (5-year) reunion receive a 50% discount on national dues, already included in this rate.

C’2020 and earlier

$100

National dues are $50 and local dues are $50.
